Case study 06 · Healthcare · Booking management

Booking management built for nurses

Careme Nurse lets nurses, nurse aides and healthcare assistants set up a professional profile, manage bookings and connect with patients. Their bookings change often, so the app has to make every change clear and quick to act on.

Role
Senior Flutter Developer
Stack
Flutter · GetX · SignalR · FCM
Platforms
iOS & Android

Get the app: App Store ↗ · Google Play ↗

01 / The setup

The newest app in the Careme family

Careme Nurse joins the patient and doctor apps on the same ASP.NET Core backend. It is built in Flutter with GetX, with live updates over SignalR and reminders over Firebase Cloud Messaging.

02 / The problem

Bookings kept changing, and both sides had to agree

A nurse might get several requests in a day, move some to a better time and cancel others. If those changes weren't clear, or didn't reach the patient quickly, both sides could end up with different plans.

03 / The fix

A clear status flow with live sync

I gave every booking a small, explicit set of actions and made each one update everyone immediately.

01

Accept, reschedule, cancel

Each booking has clear actions and states, so a nurse always knows what needs a response.

02

SignalR live updates

New requests and changes from patients appear without refreshing.

03

Day view with time slots

Bookings are grouped by day so a nurse can plan visits at a glance.

04

FCM reminders

Reminders arrive before each visit, even when the app is closed.

04 / The result

Nurses and patients share one plan

Nurses can respond to requests and change plans from one screen, and patients see each change straight away. Because it shares Careme's booking model and backend, the nurse app fits the same system as the doctor and patient apps.

3actions: accept, reschedule, cancel

3 appsin one Careme system

What I took from it: for people who work on the move, fewer and clearer choices beat more features. A small status flow made the app easy to use between visits.
