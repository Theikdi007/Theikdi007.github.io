Case study 02 · Cinema booking · Real-time

No more double-booked seats

J Cineplex lets moviegoers pick a film, choose their seats and pay in the app. On a busy opening night, many people look at the same seat map at once, so the seat you choose has to really be yours.

Role
Flutter developer
Stack
Flutter · GetX · WebView pay
Result
10,000+ bookings, month 1

Get the app: App Store ↗ · Google Play ↗

01 / The setup

From showtime to seat to payment

Users browse movies and cinemas, pick a showtime, choose seats on a live seat map and pay through a WebView payment page. The app is built in Flutter with GetX.

02 / The problem

Two people could choose the same seat

When a popular film opened, several people could pick the same seat within seconds. Without a hold on the server, both saw the seat as free, and one of them only found out at the payment step, after spending time choosing.

For a cinema that is a lost sale and an angry customer, right at the moment they were ready to pay.

03 / The fix

Hold the seat on the server, show it live to everyone

I moved the decision about who owns a seat to the server, and made every phone reflect that decision as it happened.

01

Server holds seats for a few minutes

Selecting seats creates a hold on the backend, so no one else can take them while you pay.

02

Live seat map updates

Other users see held and booked seats change on their map in real time.

03

Re-check before payment

Just before opening the payment page, the app confirms with the server that the seats are still held for this user.

04

Release on cancel or timeout

If payment fails, the user leaves, or the hold expires, the seats go back on sale automatically.

04 / The result

Seat conflicts caught before payment, not after

Users now find out about a taken seat while they are still choosing, never after paying, and abandoned selections free up seats for others on their own. The app handled more than 10,000 bookings in its first month.

10,000+bookings in the first month
Liveseat map for every user
Heldseats reserved on the server until paid

What I took from it: for anything scarce, like seats, stock or time slots, the server must own the truth and the app's job is to show that truth fast. Holds with timeouts are what make it fair.
